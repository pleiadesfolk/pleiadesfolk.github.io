--- layout: tools title: Plural and dissociative experience checklist description: An interactive checklist of common plural and dissociative experiences that you can play with. permalink: /tools/plural-checklist ---

The checklist below is adapted from The Dragonheart Collective's Am I Plural document which you can find here. Mark what feels familiar — tap or click once for a "maybe", and tap or click once for a "I experience this". You can also generate a shareable link that's useful to share with someone!

It would be remiss for us to state that this checklist isn't a diagnostic one. We can't diagnose you with a dissociative disorder. Like the Dragonheart Collective, we are not psychologists. Experiencing something on this list doesn't mean that you're definitely having DID, OSDD, or DDNOS — all of which have their own diagnostic criteria — but these are experiences that are common to those who are plural.
