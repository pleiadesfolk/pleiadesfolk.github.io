pk;l -bmc graphing tool

This is a tool that will read your system pk;l -bmc output and turn it into a nice treemap of your biggest yappers.

We say 'yappers' here since, well, -bmc only tracks message count. Your biggest fronter might not yap a lot! They might be quiet and just not do anything!

Message count data also doesn't take into account how verbose your headmates are. If your headmate talks in long paragraphs, they might be represented less here.

Either way, here's how to use this tool:

      1. First, go to PluralKit, and type in pk;l -bmc.
      2. Paste the output into the text field below.
      3. If you have multiple pages of output, continue down these pages until you have all your system members in one list.
      4. Hit Render.
      5. If you have a large system or an uneven system, you can hit "log scale" to visualise the smaller (more quieter) alters in your system.
      6. You can click on a proxy to remove a member from the list.
      7. You can also use the scroll wheel or pinch to zoom and navigate.
