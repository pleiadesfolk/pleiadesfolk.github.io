Printing and folding your zine

instructions for turning your exported PDF into a pocket-sized booklet

So, now that you have your typed-up zine from the Field Psychological Safety Toolkit, here's how you can make it into something pocket-sized!

You'll need

  1. To get the zine printed. If you don't have a printer, a local library would help! Remember, the zine is A4-sized — you may need to resize it if the paper you usually use is smaller (or, US Letter; some cropping may occur on Letter sizes. sorgy)
  2. A pair of scissors.
  3. A stapler (or if you prefer, string and needle)

Steps

  1. Fold your zine lengthwise.
  2. Fold it in half again…
  3. and again…
  4. Until your zine has four folds (one lengthwise; three width-wise), like the diagram you see above.
  5. Fold the paper in half. Cut along the black dash — make sure not to cut beyond that!
  6. You'll get a piece of paper with a cut in the middle.
  7. Fold it lengthwise again… and this time round you'll get an accordion shape. Push it in like the diagram above.
  8. See the front and back pages? Fold it so that they're the front and back page. Secure with staples on the grey dashed portions (they're marked!).

Optional things you can do

The zine will start parting along the bottom, where sheets aren't secured. If you have tape, you can carefully tape up the bottom of the zine before you staple it together.

Additionally, you can use string to bind your zine together. You can find instructions on how you can do it here.
